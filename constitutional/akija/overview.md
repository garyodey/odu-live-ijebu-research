# AKIJA of Ikija — Constitutional Overview

## Office identity

**AKIJA** is treated here as the indigenous office attached to **Ikija**. The office must be kept separate from:

- the office-holder,
- personal/regnal names,
- styles such as "His Highness",
- later use of "Oba",
- the place Ikija itself.

## Documented succession structure

The Akija chieftaincy declaration records four ruling houses:

1. EGBEDI
2. LUMOKAN
3. OJIGI
4. AJASUN

The same instrument records a succession process involving the ruling house, kingmakers, public proclamation, and Awujale consent.

## Kingmaking bodies

The declaration records kingmakers from four institutional streams:

- ILAMUREN
- PAMPA
- OSUGBO
- ODI
