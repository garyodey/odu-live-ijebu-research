# AKIJA Kingmakers

The working transcription from the Akija declaration identifies eight kingmakers:

| Office | Institutional body | Recorded function |
|---|---|---|
| OLISA OF IKIJA | ILAMUREN | Head of Ilamuren; second in rank to Akija |
| BALATO OF IKIJA | ILAMUREN | Ilamuren; third in rank to Akija |
| SUTIRADE OF IKIJA | ILAMUREN | Ilamuren; fourth in rank to Akija |
| AGBON OF IKIJA | PAMPA | Head of Pampa |
| KAKANFO OF IKIJA | PAMPA | Pampa |
| OLIWO OF IKIJA | OSUGBO | Head of Osugbo |
| OLOTU APENA | OSUGBO | Member of Osugbo |
| OGBENI ODI OF IKIJA | ODI | Head of the Odis |

## Research caution

The constitutional bodies are not assumed to be identical in origin, territorial basis, ritual function, or social composition. Their convergence in kingmaking does not collapse their institutional identities.
