# ODI

The ODI node must remain multi-functional until the historical record is reconstructed.

The Akija declaration records an **Ogbeni Odi of Ikija** as **head of the Odis** and includes that office among the kingmakers.

This establishes that, at least in the codified Akija succession system, ODI cannot be reduced to a purely non-constitutional "attendant" category.

Possible functions to test independently:

- palace/service function
- constitutional/kingmaking function
- ritual function
- lineage or corporate identity
- executive function

`SERVICE_FUNCTION != ABSENCE_OF_CONSTITUTIONAL_POWER`
