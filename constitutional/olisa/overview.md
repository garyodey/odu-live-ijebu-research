# OLISA

## Separate office nodes

At minimum preserve:

- `OLISA[IJEBU-ODE]`
- `OLISA[IKIJA]`

Same office-name does not imply same office-entity.

## Ikija

The Akija declaration documents an **Olisa of Ikija** as head of Ilamuren and second in rank to the Akija within the succession framework.

## Ijebu-Ode

Modern disputes about whether the Olisa of Ijebu-Ode is styled/classified as an Oba should not erase OLISA as the underlying indigenous office. Treat "Oba" as a potentially context-dependent classification or style unless the relevant legal instrument establishes otherwise.
