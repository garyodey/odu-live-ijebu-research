# IPEBI — Lexical and Functional Evidence

Status: **FUNCTIONAL EVIDENCE; IKIJA APPLICATION UNRESOLVED**

## Lexical evidence

A Yoruba transcript dictionary indexed online distinguishes two visually similar forms in its transcription system:

- **ipebbil** — "place where kings are taken in for initiation few days prior to being crowned"
- **ipebi** — "starvation"

The orthography used by this dictionary is non-standard/transcriptional and must not be silently converted into modern Yoruba spelling. Nevertheless, it independently confirms that a form represented approximately as **ipebi** can denote a royal pre-coronation initiation place.

Evidence state: **LEXICAL_FUNCTIONAL_CONTROL**

## Historical Ikija geography

A Government of Western Nigeria election instrument lists:

- Ward 26: **Ikija : Ilede Ipebi**
- Ward 27: **Ikija, Ilenla, Odomefi**

This establishes IPEBI and ILENLA as historical territorial labels within Ikija's administrative geography.

Evidence state: **DOCUMENTED HISTORICAL GEOGRAPHY**

## Combined inference

The combination of:

1. a documented historical IPEBI place-name in Ikija, and
2. independent lexical/historical evidence for IPEBI as a royal initiation/pre-coronation institution

raises the historical plausibility that **IPEBI[IKIJA]** could preserve an installation-related function.

It does **not** establish that function for Ikija.

## Evidence threshold for upgrade

Require direct Ikija evidence connecting any of the following:

```text
AKIJA-ELECT
   ↓
IPEBI[IKIJA]
   ↓
INITIATION / SECLUSION / INSTALLATION
   ↓
AKIJA
```

Acceptable upgrade evidence includes an installation narrative, chieftaincy declaration, archival administrative report, map identifying the installation site, or independently corroborated oral testimony.
