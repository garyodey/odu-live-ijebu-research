# ULE / ILE / ILẸ̀

## Working distinction

- **ulé** — documented Ijebu form for house/home in linguistic sources
- **ilé** — Standard Yoruba house/home
- **ilẹ̀** — land/ground

Unmarked `ILE` can conceal distinct Yoruba forms. Tone and vowel quality are identity-relevant.

## Method

For every occurrence record:

- SOURCE_FORM
- PERIOD
- DIALECT / LOCATION
- TONE / DIACRITIC FORM (if reliable)
- FUNCTION IN CONTEXT
- NORMALIZED_FORM (if any)
- EVIDENCE_STATE
