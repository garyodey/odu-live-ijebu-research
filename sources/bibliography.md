# Working Bibliography

## Primary / legal / archival targets

- Western Region / Western State Chieftaincy Declarations, Ijebu Division (Chiefs Law framework, 1957 and later reproductions).
  - https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/26/WESTERN_STATE_CHIEFTAINCY_DECLARATIONS_IJEBU_DIVISION%2C_%281957%29.pdf
- National Archives Ibadan, File C17/5/2 — genealogical material associated with Fusengbuwa (1944; CTC reportedly 2026).
- National Archives Ibadan, File C17/7 — Affairs upon the Awujale / Oduwole Olufadi petition material (1934).
- Ogun State High Court, Ijebu-Ode Division, Suit HCJ/149/2026 — current pleadings; pleadings are not findings.

## Public secondary sources used as pointers

- Punch Nigeria — reports on Fusengbuwa Ruling House / Ile Nla / Agunsebi.
  - https://punchng.com/
- The Nation — reports reproducing Ile-Nla / Agunsebi address and succession materials.
  - https://thenationonlineng.net/
- Guardian Nigeria — interviews and historical succession claims.
  - https://guardian.ng/
- University of Ibadan repository — Ijebu linguistic research.
  - https://repository.ui.edu.ng/
- University of Edinburgh repository — historical/linguistic studies of Yoruba/Ijebu.
  - https://era.ed.ac.uk/

## Citation policy

Prefer the primary instrument or archival document over a newspaper paraphrase whenever available. When a source makes a legal or genealogical claim, record the claim separately from the document's authenticity.
