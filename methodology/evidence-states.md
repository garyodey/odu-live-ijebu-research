# Evidence States

- **DOCUMENTED** — directly supported by a primary or authoritative source.
- **SOURCE_CLAIM** — asserted by a source but not independently established.
- **ORAL_TRADITION** — preserved oral account; source and provenance should be recorded.
- **LINGUISTIC_HYPOTHESIS** — plausible linguistic interpretation requiring additional evidence.
- **G_MATCH** — graphemic/structural overlap only; no identity or etymology implied.
- **V_ORTH** — documented orthographic variant.
- **T_LING** — independently supported linguistic/dialectal correspondence.
- **DISPUTED** — materially contested by competing sources or litigants.
- **UNRESOLVED** — insufficient evidence to determine.
- **JUDICIAL_FINDING** — expressly decided by a court; must cite the judgment and proposition.

## Rule

Evidence state attaches to the proposition, not merely to the document. A genuine document can contain disputed or false claims.
