# Linguistic Rules

1. Preserve the full source form before segmentation.
2. Preserve tone marks, underdots, and dialect spellings when available.
3. `ILE` is unresolved when the source omits diacritics: it may conceal **ilé** (house/home), **ilẹ̀** (land/ground), or another local form.
4. Ijebu **ulé** may correspond to Standard Yoruba **ilé**; treat this as a dialectal correspondence only when independently supported.
5. English-readable substrings inside Yoruba/Ijebu names do not acquire English meaning automatically.
6. Graphemic reversal, substring overlap, and letter rotation remain G-level observations until historical/linguistic evidence exists.
