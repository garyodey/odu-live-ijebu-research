# Identity and Topology Rules

## Entity separation

Preserve distinct nodes for:

- PERSON
- OFFICE
- PLACE
- RULING_HOUSE
- LINEAGE / EBI
- COMPOUND / ULE
- QUARTER
- STYLE / HONORIFIC
- ADMINISTRATIVE_CLASSIFICATION

## Typed relationships

Prefer explicit relations such as:

- HOLDS_OFFICE
- DESCENDS_FROM
- MEMBER_OF
- NOMINATES
- SELECTS
- HEADS
- ASSEMBLES_AT
- LOCATED_IN
- CONSENTS_TO
- ASSOCIATED_WITH

Never infer an identity edge merely from spelling similarity, shared location, title usage, or membership-card evidence.
