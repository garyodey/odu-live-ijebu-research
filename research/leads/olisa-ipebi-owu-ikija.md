# Research Lead — OLISA / IPEBI / OWU-IKIJA

Status: **RESEARCH LEAD — NOT YET CONSTITUTIONAL FACT**

## 1. Owu-Ikija / Olisa territorial claim

A surfaced copy of a history inaugural lecture states that a second wave of Owu migrants established **Owu-Ikija in 1854** on territory said to have previously belonged to the **Olisa of Ikija**.

Evidence state: **SOURCE_CLAIM**

This is potentially important because it independently places the OLISA OF IKIJA node in a territorial relationship, not merely a rank/title relationship.

Required next step: identify the original lecture, author, publication details, footnotes and underlying source for the 1854 proposition before upgrading.

## 2. Isale Ipebi / Olisa Street

Multiple web-indexed commercial posts from 2013–2020 give an address in Ikija-Ijebu as:

**Olisa Street / Isale Ipebi / Ikija-Ijebu**

One form is: **3, Olisa Street, Isale Ipebi, Ikija-Ijebu**.

Evidence state: **LOW-AUTHORITY LOCATION LEAD**

This is not evidence of the age, constitutional function or origin of Ipebi. It is evidence that the toponyms **OLISA** and **IPEBI** co-occur in modern address usage at Ikija.

## 3. Government geographic lead

An Ogun State 2012 rural water/sanitation budget lists both **Ipebi** and **Owu Ikija** under Ijebu East LGA rehabilitation locations.

Evidence state: **DOCUMENTED MODERN TOPONYM**

This independently confirms IPEBI and OWU IKIJA as modern geographic labels in the relevant local-government area, but does not by itself establish their historical constitutional relationship.
