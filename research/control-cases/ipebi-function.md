# IPEBI — Functional Control Cases

Status: **COMPARATIVE FUNCTIONAL EVIDENCE**

## Why this file exists

The existence of a modern place-name `Isale Ipebi` at Ikija does not by itself prove that the place housed an installation institution. To test the hypothesis, compare independently documented Ipebi functions elsewhere in Ijebu/Yoruba constitutional practice.

## Control case A — Alake of Egbaland, 1972

A National Library-hosted historical account records that the Alake-elect, Prince Mofolorunso Oyebade Lipede, was **put into Ipebi for traditional ceremonies preparatory to crowning and installation** on 20 May 1972.

Evidence state: **DOCUMENTED FUNCTIONAL CONTROL**

This demonstrates IPEBI functioning as a pre-installation/seclusion stage in a kingship process outside Ikija.

## Control case B — Awujale, 1960

Published recollections of Awujale Sikiru Adetona's installation describe the young king-elect as being at **Ipebi** in late March 1960 while being prepared for crowning.

Evidence state: **SOURCE_CLAIM / STRONG CONTEMPORARY-STYLE RECOLLECTION**

## Control case C — Olisa of Ijebu-Ode

A 2026 historical essay on the Olisa succession states that an Olisa-elect was sent to the traditional **Ipebi Olisa at Afokoro/Ijada** for prescribed seclusion before proceeding to Ilisa.

Evidence state: **SOURCE_CLAIM — requires primary archival corroboration**

## Ikija evidence

Modern address usage independently records **Olisa Street, Isale Ipebi, Ikija-Ijebu**.

Ogun State's 2012 RUWATSAN budget independently lists **Ipebi** as a community/location in Ijebu East LGA.

Evidence state:
- IPEBI as modern geographic label: **DOCUMENTED**
- Isale Ipebi + Olisa Street co-location: **LOW-AUTHORITY LOCATION LEAD**
- IPEBI as historical Akija installation institution: **UNRESOLVED**

## Functional hypothesis

```text
KING / TITLE HOLDER ELECT
        ↓
      IPEBI
[seclusion / preparation /
 installation-transition]
        ↓
PUBLIC / CONSTITUTIONAL OFFICE
```

The control cases show that this function is historically plausible. They do **not** prove that Ikija's Ipebi had the same function.

## Research test

To upgrade IPEBI[IKIJA], locate one of:

1. Akija declaration language explicitly naming Ipebi;
2. installation account for an Akija naming Ipebi;
3. archival map or administrative record identifying an Ipebi house/site;
4. oral testimony with named custodians, rites, and location that can be independently cross-checked;
5. older document connecting Olisa of Ikija, kingmakers, or Akija-elect to Ipebi.
