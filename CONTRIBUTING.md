# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they preserve provenance and evidence state.

## Minimum requirements

Every substantive historical proposition should identify:

1. the proposition,
2. source,
3. source date or period where known,
4. evidence state,
5. whether the statement is fact, source claim, oral tradition, interpretation, or unresolved.

Do not convert allegations in pleadings, newspapers, Facebook posts, or oral accounts into established facts.

## Identity discipline

Keep PERSON, OFFICE, PLACE, LINEAGE, RULING_HOUSE, COMPOUND, STYLE and ADMINISTRATIVE_CLASSIFICATION separate.

## Yoruba / Ijebu forms

Preserve the complete source spelling before normalization. Do not silently add or remove tone marks, underdots, H, N, U/I, or other features.

## Corrections

Corrections should preserve the superseded form in the evidence history when historically useful rather than silently rewriting the record.
