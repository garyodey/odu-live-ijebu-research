# Ikija Electoral Geography — Ipebi and Ilenla

Status: **DOCUMENTED HISTORICAL GEOGRAPHY**

## Source

A Government of Western Nigeria local-government election instrument preserved by the National Library of Nigeria lists wards in the **Owu-Ikija Group**.

The relevant entries are:

- **Ward 23:** Owu — Imayan, Imagbon
- **Ward 24:** Owu — Ebailela, Ibese
- **Ward 25:** Iganmeji, Isire, Isomu, Ifodo, Agerige, Togumaga
- **Ward 26:** **Ikija — Ilede Ipebi**
- **Ward 27:** **Ikija, Ilenla, Odomefi**

## Significance

This is substantially stronger than the modern commercial-address evidence.

It establishes that:

1. **IPEBI** was embedded in the historical administrative/electoral geography of Ikija, not merely a recent informal address.
2. **ILENLA** was independently named in the same Ikija electoral structure.
3. IPEBI and ILENLA were represented in **different ward entries**, so they must not be collapsed into a single territorial node.
4. Ikija sat within an **Owu-Ikija Group** that also contained Owu wards and additional named settlements/areas.

## Unresolved transcription

The source extraction renders Ward 26 as **"Ilede Ipebi"**. This must be checked against the page image/original typography before deciding whether the source form is:

- ILEDE IPEBI,
- ILEDE/IPEBI as two items,
- or another orthographic reading.

Do not normalize this string prematurely.

## Working topology

```text
OWU-IKIJA GROUP
├── OWU wards
├── other named areas
└── IKIJA
    ├── [ILEDE?] IPEBI   (Ward 26)
    └── ILENLA + ODOMEFI (Ward 27)
```

## What this does NOT prove

The electoral record does not establish:

- that IPEBI was an Akija installation house;
- that ILENLA supplied a ruling house or kingmaker;
- that IPEBI and OLISA were constitutionally linked;
- the age or etymology of either place-name.

Those remain separate research questions.
